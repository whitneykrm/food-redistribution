Good food. Greater impact.

Food Connect links Nairobi's food vendors with people who want good food at a fair price, so surplus meals reach a plate instead of a bin.

Why we built it

Every day, vendors across the city end up with perfectly good food they can't sell before it spoils. Today that surplus is usually handled informally: through end-of-day discounts, word of mouth, or simply thrown away. At the same time, many households are looking for affordable meals.

Food Connect gives that surplus a proper channel. Vendors list what they have left, and consumers reserve it at a discount and collect it before it expires. Everyone benefits: vendors recover some of their cost, consumers save money, and less food goes to waste.

How it works

1

Vendors list surplus

Verified vendors post their surplus food with a discounted price, quantity and expiry time.

2

Consumers reserve

Browse listings, search by food, vendor or location, and reserve what you want before it expires.

3

Collect with a QR code

Every reservation comes with a unique QR code. Show it to the vendor when you collect your order.

4

Earn points

Each collected order earns you points, which you can later redeem for more surplus food.

What we stand for

Less waste

Food that is still good should be eaten. Every rescued meal is one less thrown away.

Trust

Vendors are verified, and QR claim codes make every handover traceable and secure.

Access

Discounted meals make good food more affordable for the people in our community.
